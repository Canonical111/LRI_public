## Method

1. **Definitions and framework** (phase 3, `framework/`). C8 = 8 T4 is affine in the ten new inputs; the framework derives eq:C8assembly, C8 = B_old + 4W + 2S + 3/4 U + 1/4 J9 + 3/32 L3 - 1/4 M21 + 1/2 M12 - 1/16 N2 + 1/8 Ndelta + 1/32 Kv, from J0 beta = R1 through g^9 and the delta^4 trace. It reproduces the appendix polynomial B_old (61 terms) and the note's coefficients exactly (`framework/derive_C8.py`).
2. **Engine tier A** (`engine/PRODUCTION_C8.md`). 21 jobs (19 groups) give the 26 raw scalars the seven tier-A inputs need beyond the 116 phase-2 scalars. The A3_ddE job was rerun at 2N with fresh seeds (top-up). R = 6 random shifts per job, seeds disjoint from every earlier campaign. The run used the personal desktop, because symmetry was down.
3. **Assembly** (`assembly/run_assembly_C8.py`, this phase). The phase-2 production input (116 scalars) and the tier-A input (26 scalars) are merged as independent runs (142 inputs, 96 replicate blocks, R = 6). `Framework("C8").from_replicates` is then run in **strict mode on every reported quantity**: the 25 old inputs, C3...C7, the seven tier-A inputs, B_old and B_done must have every raw scalar they need, or the run stops. U, J9, Kv, C8 = 8 T4 and eq:C8assembly are reported MISSING, with the raw scalars they lack. C8_partial = B_old + the seven tier-A terms (= B_done + Ndelta/8).
4. **Error estimators** (as in phase 2, each including the engine precision floor 1e-15 x L1 per coefficient):
   * `sigma_cov`: linear propagation with exact gradients and the per-job replicate covariance;
   * `sigma_rep`: standard error of the six per-replicate assemblies (5 dof);
   * `sigma_pool`: sector-pooled projection of every job on the output gradient (hundreds of dof for the multi-sector jobs);
   * the quoted `sigma` is the largest of the three.
5. **Checks** (archive-free): `engine/tests/validate_C8.py` and `engine/tests/direct_C8.py`, rerun here with tier A listed first (outputs in `assembly/out/C8/`); tier-to-tier consistency; W three ways; the note's raw structure of Ndelta.
6. **Comparison** (`assembly/compare_archive_C8.py`). It refuses to run unless `RESULTS_C8_independent.json` exists and still has its recorded SHA-256, and it verifies `spec/comparison_C8/SHA256SUMS`. Only then does it read the sealed `archive_C8_values.json`.

## Limitations

* **C8 is not tested.** U, J9 and Kv need tiers B and C (next section). The hybrid comparison only shows that the archive's C8 is consistent with our B_old and tier-A terms.
* **Errors are statistical estimates.** The QMC errors are not rigorous bounds. R = 6 shifts per job gives 5 dof for sigma_rep. The sector-pooled estimator has many dof only for jobs whose variance is spread over many sectors; it has few for the 3-kink endpoint jobs (A3_ddE, and the phase-2 A3_vvE, A3_dE that dominate N2 and Ndelta). The S comparison (2.1 sigma) suggests that sigma(S) may be low by a factor of about 2.
* **sigma(B_done) and sigma(C8_partial) come from phase 2.** They are about 1.2e-5, set by B_old (J, K, Omega; jobs A5_E, Q8, A7, Z4_E). Tier A contributes 2e-8 and 2e-7. A tier-2 rerun of the phase-2 groups (`RESULTS.md`, Limitations) would be needed to go below 1e-5.
* **One strict re-check is missing.** The Q6_vd sector strict re-check of criterion 5 did not complete (engine open item). Q6_vd carries Ndelta's tier-A error (2.4e-6 pooled, against 9e-6 total). Q6_vv, which has the same geometry, passed at 5.6e-17 of the sector size.
* **The direct finite-cutoff test is coarse** for the new moments (3-sigma sizes 1e-4..1e4 absolute). It checks conventions, not the single logs at the QMC precision. The finer archive-free evidence is internal: the explicit L3, M21, N2 identities, the higher logs, the pole cancellation and tier-to-tier consistency.
* **Tier A ran over its cap in raw core-hours.** It ran on the personal desktop; 183.1 raw personal-desktop core-h exceeds the 156 core-h cap, while the amdq-equivalent 122.2 does not (engine open item 1).
