## Method in brief

1. **Definitions** (`spec/`): the 25 inputs (a, p, A, E, c, f, b, G, q, k, V, R, J, L, M, N, alpha, beta, gamma, Phi, Psi, Omega, Q, P, K) as Taylor coefficients of the auxiliary beta functions b3...b7, h4...h8 of the note's finite-response recurrence, written from the note alone.
2. **Framework** (`framework/`): the response-to-beta map J0 beta = R1, derived from first principles and identical to the note; normalized responses formed before extraction; the fixed point and the invariants T, D; C3...C7 = conversion of D2...D4, T2, T3. Strict mode: all 116 required raw coefficients must be supplied.
3. **Engine** (`engine/`): a from-scratch numerical engine for the eps-expansion of the hard-core ordered moments: Mellin transform in the cutoff, Hepp sectors with analytic subtraction of the divergent variables, rank-1 lattice QMC with R = 6 independent random shifts per job. Tier-1 production on symmetry amdq (2942.9 core-h including checks): 116 needed coefficients from 2 to 8 kinks, the four groups dominating C7 topped up with fresh seeds.
4. **Assembly** (`assembly/`, this phase): the production replicates through the framework per replicate (strict); three error estimators; the archive-free checks; three small engine runs that feed only the checks, never an input (`engine/runs/poles1`: negative eps powers for the complete pole-cancellation check; `engine/runs/kplus1`: eps^1 coefficients for complete direct-comparison series; `engine/runs/conv1`: a convergence re-check of the three moments the direct test flags); then the comparison.
5. **Direct** (`direct/`): the raw moments evaluated directly at 25 finite cutoffs (nested Gauss-Legendre, ~1e-14 relative), compared with the engine series by a generalized least-squares test.

**Error estimators** (all include the engine precision floor 1e-15 x L1 per coefficient):
* `sigma_cov`: linear propagation with the exact framework gradients and the replicate covariance of each job;
* `sigma_rep`: standard error of the six full per-replicate assemblies (5 dof);
* `sigma_pool`: sector-pooled: each job's per-sector, per-shift values projected on the gradient, sum over sectors of var/R (hundreds of dof);
* quoted `sigma` = max of the three. Pulls below use it unless stated otherwise.

## Limitations

* The reference error should be the sector-pooled one (hundreds to thousands of effective dof). sigma_rep has exactly 5 dof and sigma_cov about 5.5 (C7) or 8.6 (C6), because C7 is 95% Q8 and C6 75% A5_E. The quoted sigma (max of the three) is therefore conservative but itself uncertain by about 30%.
* Aggregate rms pulls mix two regimes. Checks dominated by the precision floor have rms 0.05-0.26 and test exact structure. Checks dominated by statistical error behave as N(0,1): chi2/dof 0.42-1.30 (statistics verifier).
* Two direct-test flags would matter if real. A5_vv[eps^0 l^0] shows a window-stable direct offset of about -1.5e-4 (2.3 sigma). If real, it would move Omega by -4x that and C7, D4 by about 2.4 sigma. A3_vdE[eps^0 l^0] gives d0 of about +6e-5 in alternative windows, about 1.9 sigma of C7 at face value, but its sign flips between windows. Both are ruled out by the convergence re-check (conv1, stable to 4e-8) and by the step-3 archive agreement of Omega and Psi. Without that archive comparison, the archive-free evidence on these two coefficients is weaker than the C6/C7 pulls alone suggest.
* C7 and D4 are effectively one test, dominated by K (Q8). C6 is the T3 test and C5 the D3 test.
* The QMC errors are statistical estimates, not rigorous bounds. Each job has R = 6 random shifts, so its replicate error has 5 degrees of freedom (Student t tails; single-coefficient pulls of 3 are not rare). The sector-pooled estimator has many more degrees of freedom but assumes the sectors' shifts are independent (true by construction) and that the per-sector variance is estimated without bias.
* The precision floor (1e-15 of the L1 size of each coefficient) is set a priori; the strict-tolerance re-evaluations of the most-warned sectors (engine, PRODUCTION_TIER1.md) found no systematic effect above 1.5e-13 of a sector, i.e. far below the statistical errors.
* Lattice rules for the 6-dimensional Q8 and Q6_E sectors converge slowly and pre-asymptotically at small N; their error estimates are the least certain. Q8 dominates sigma(C7) (~3e-5) and K.
* The direct finite-cutoff comparison resolves the 3- to 6-kink coefficients (completed K = 1 series), but for A7, A7_v and Q8 it is only a gross-error screen. Their eps^1 runs (~400 core-h) were not made, and even then it would stay a screen at the 1e-3..1e-2 level. The evidence for the 7- and 8-kink inputs is internal: the higher logs (l^2..l^8, same jobs as the needed l^1), the RG second-log check of C7, K8, the pole cancellation of C7, C7_v, K8, and tier-1/top-up consistency.
* The RG second-log check holds only at (v, delta) = (1, 0): away from it the matching adds explicit logs that the auxiliary-coupling formula does not contain.
* Part of the pole-cancellation check (106 coefficients of A3_d, A3_vd, A3_vv, A5, A5_d, A5_v, Q4_d, Q4_v, Q4_vd, Q4_vv, Q6, Z4_d, Z6, Z6_v at negative eps powers) rests on the engine's phase-1 validation runs. Their errors come from quadrature and are up to 2x too small for some multi-channel coefficients.
* Production errors of single coefficients can be low: the direct comparison exposed Z4_E[eps^-1 l^0] 4 replicate sigma (1.6 pooled) off the converged value. The quoted output errors (max of three estimators) absorb such cases where they matter: the effect here is 7e-10 on C7.
* Tier 2 (planned in engine/README.md, ~19k core-h conservatively, likely several times less at the measured Q8 rate) would bring sigma(C6) to ~1e-8 and sigma(C7) to ~2e-7 (Q8 at N >= 524287, R = 8 for more replicate dof), turning the present 6-digit C7 test into an 8-9 digit one. It would also allow complete K >= 1 series for the 5-8 kink direct comparison.
