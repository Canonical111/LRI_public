3. **Assembly** (`assembly/run_assembly_C8.py`). Production + `engine/runs/c8tiersABC/framework_input_final.json` (the three tiers merged per scalar) as independent runs: 201 inputs, 141 replicate blocks, R = 6. `Framework('C8', dps=50).from_replicates` in **strict mode on every output**: the 35 inputs, C3...C8, T4, B_old, B_done and C8_asm need 201 raw scalars and all are supplied. The three tier files fed separately give identical values and linear errors. C8 is computed both as 8 T4 (fixed point) and as eq:C8assembly; they differ by 1.7e-13, the effect of the engine's tree datum A3[eps^0 l^1] = 6 to double precision.
4. **Sub-pieces.** U, Kv, J9 (and Ndelta, C_fixed) are split as in the note (l.1344-1347, 1363-1366, 1388-1391, eq:NdP6, eq:C8short) into raw coefficients and remainders. Each is a linear functional of the framework outputs and the raw scalars, with exact gradients and per-replicate values; the note's coefficients (+1, -1, +2, -2; -4, +16, -8; -4, +16, -8) are reproduced exactly by the framework gradients, and every remainder has zero gradient on the raw coefficients split off.
5. **Error estimators** (as in phases 2 and 3, each including the engine precision floor 1e-15 x L1 per coefficient):
   * `sigma_cov`: linear propagation with exact gradients and the per-job replicate covariance;
   * `sigma_rep`: standard error of the six per-replicate assemblies (5 dof);
   * `sigma_pool`: sector-pooled projection of every job on the output gradient (many dof for multi-sector jobs);
   * the quoted `sigma` is the largest of the three. `sigma_jobmax` (per job the larger of its replicate and pooled projections, as in the engine's `budget_C8.py`) is reported for reference.
6. **Archive-free checks** (`assembly/slurm/assembly_C8_personal-desktop.sbatch`, one personal-desktop Slurm job): `engine/tests/validate_C8.py` and `engine/tests/direct_C8.py` with the production tiers listed first, `assembly/direct_probe_C8.py` (fit-window probe of the flagged direct moments), then `run_assembly_C8.py`. ## What the C8 test shows

* **Precision.** sigma(C8) = 2.37, i.e. 1.0 % of C8. This was the approved target for tier C (a gross check at sigma(C8) ~ 3). Everything except the J9 term is known to 5.1e-03 (2e-05 of C8); J9 itself to 3.2 %, set by the 7D integral A9[eps^0 l^1] at N = 8009 (its absolute error 9.3 sits on a value of -6.1e5, 1.5e-5 relative).
* **Agreement.** Our C8 is 1.9 sigma below the claim with the quoted error (3.3-4.0 sigma with the smaller linear and replicate estimators), and the whole difference is A9_1. The parts of the archive's C8 that we test sharply, B_old, the seven tier-A inputs, U and Kv, agree at the 1e-5 level of C8 (C8 - J9/4: -0.20 sigma at 5.1e-03). So the archive's assembly of C8, its U and its Kv are confirmed (3e-07 and 3e-05 relative); its J9 is confirmed only to our 3 % error and differs from ours by 6.7 % (-1.92 sigma), entirely through A9_1 (3.3e-05 relative of A9_1).
* **What would settle A9_1.** A second, independent A9[eps^0 l^1] run with fresh seeds (about 9k amdq core-h at N = 8009, or more at a higher N) would test our A9 error estimate directly. It does not fit in the remaining cap of this phase.
* **The zeta formula is not proved by this.** The candidate closed form has eleven rational coefficients on zeta values of weight <= 7. A 1 % measurement of C8 is consistent with it and with any other number within a few units; it cannot identify the coefficients. Even the archive's 26-digit C8 only makes the closed form a numerical identification (PSLQ-type), and our test reaches about 2 digits of C8. To test the closed form at a meaningful level, J9 (i.e. A9[eps^0 l^1]) would have to be computed to about 1e-9 relative (sigma(C8) ~ 1e-4), which with the present method is far out of reach (the engine plan needs about 1.3e5 amdq core-h for sigma(C8) = 0.01 at an assumed rate 2, and 1e-4 would need about ten times more points), so it needs a better integrand for A9.

## Limitations

* **C8 is a 1 % test.** sigma(C8) = 2.37 comes from A9[eps^0 l^1] alone (93 %). The replicate estimator of A9 is 0.52 of the pooled one; with the replicate or linear error (1.15, 1.37) the C8 pull would be -3.98 / -3.32. The pooled estimator (thousands of sector dof) is the more reliable one and is quoted; the replicate estimator has 5 dof. Two independent comparisons involving A9 deviate by about 2.2 sigma (the probe sample, archive-free, and the archive's A9_1), so sigma(A9) may be underestimated; an independent A9 rerun would settle it.
* **Errors are statistical estimates**, not rigorous bounds. A9 and A7_E have independent references only on sector samples (the probe and the conv pilots); A7_v[eps^0 l^0] and A7[eps^1 l^1] were rerun in this phase with fresh seeds.
* **The 6-9 kink direct tests are coarse** (gross-error screens); their flags here are tail-model dependent.
* **Criterion 5 is open for two items**: the Q8_v strict re-check has no result (job cancelled from outside), and the tier-C heavy WARN fraction is 1.03 % against 1 % (the A9 strict re-check shows the warnings are cosmetic).
* **The cap conversion** of the personal desktop and defq core-h (0.667) is uncalibrated; the probe measured about 0.8 for 6-7D work.
* **S** stays at +2.1 sigma from the archive record (7.5e-12 relative, tier A; irrelevant for C8).
