# assembly: from the engine's production data to the beta inputs, C5, C6, D4, C7, and the checks

Part of the independent re-derivation of the C6/C7 inputs of `LRI_SRI_research_compendium` (read-only).
This directory runs the last phase. It
1. feeds the engine's production replicates through the framework (strict mode, per-replicate assembly);
2. runs every check that needs no archive number;
3. only then compares with the archive and with the claimed closed forms;
4. writes the report `../RESULTS.md`.

The headline results and the full discussion are in **`../RESULTS.md`**. In brief (2026-10-03):
* C6 = -58.6656986 +- 1.5e-6 (claim -58.66569747, pull -0.78);
* D4 = -2.5346305 +- 7.9e-6 (pull -0.09);
* C7 = 51.068986 +- 3.1e-5 (pull -0.08);
* every archive-free check passes, and all 25 inputs agree with the archive (rms pull 0.34).

The machine-readable results are `../RESULTS_independent.json` (steps 1-2, archive-free) and
`../RESULTS_archive_comparison.json` (step 3).

## Step 1: the assembly

* Input: `engine/runs/prod_6digit/framework_input_final.json` (tier 1 with the top-up groups Q6_E, A7_v,
  A7_base, A5_vE; 116 required scalars, R = 6 replicates in 75 independent blocks, 3 exact structural zeros).
  Tier 1 alone (`framework_input.json`) is assembled as a cross-check; the two differ only through the four
  replaced groups, and the table gives the pull of the difference against the errors of those groups.
* `Framework("D4", dps=50).from_replicates(...)`, strict. For every output (25 inputs, D2, T2, D3, T3, D4,
  C3...C7) three error estimates are reported, each including the engine's precision floors:
  * `sigma_cov`: linear propagation with the per-job replicate covariance (framework `sigma_linear`);
  * `sigma_rep`: standard error of the six per-replicate assemblies (framework `sigma_rep_total`);
  * `sigma_pool`: the sector-pooled estimator (`pooled_errors.py`): every job's per-sector, per-shift values
    (`per_sector[].rep` of the engine outputs, through the engine's own `coefficient_map`) projected on the
    output's gradient, `sum_s var_r / R` summed over sectors (hundreds of degrees of freedom).
  The quoted error `sigma` is the largest of the three. With R = 6 the replicate errors have 5 dof.
* Three-kink sector: the framework's semi-analytic 1D boundary integrals
  (`framework/validate_low_order.three_kink_single_log`, recomputed here at 40 digits) give a, p, A, E, V,
  alpha, beta, gamma independently of the engine.

## Step 2: checks without the archive

* **(i) closed forms the note proves**: a, p, A, E, c, b, f (eq:oldbetas, eq:Eexact); D2, T2, C3, C4; the
  periods P4 (from K4[l^0], and from the raw Q4[eps^0 l^0] through the Mellin data lo:eq:AQ), S5 (from
  C5[l^1] and from b, lo:eq:bperiod), R4 (from f and P4, lo:eq:fperiod, and from the raw d_v Q4[eps^0 l^1]
  through lo:eq:AQv). The Mellin map is checked first on the P- and R4-independent logs.
  The claims under test (D3/C5, T3/C6, D4, C7) and calR are deliberately left to step 3.
* **(ii) pole cancellation**: every coefficient [eps^p l^k], p < 0, of every normalized jet entry C_n, K_n the
  framework uses (all derivative slots, 37 entries), with all three error estimates.
  * Production computed only the eps powers the beta inputs need, so on production data alone only C3, C3_v
    and K4 can be tested.
  * **`engine/runs/poles1`** (this phase; spec `engine/specs/poles_check1.json`) computed the missing negative
    eps powers of 29 moments (45 jobs, all log powers, R = 6, fresh tier name and seeds) on symmetry amdq.
  * The complete check uses, per coefficient, production, else poles1, else the engine's earlier validation
    runs (`engine/results/validation_{d_inputs,c}.json`, phase-1 errors).
* **(iii) higher logs**: every normalized and endpoint target of `spec/out/validation_targets.json`
  (provenance `note`, `note-implied`, `note-closed-forms`), which includes all of the note's higher-log
  predictions (l.719-731: [l^6..l^4] C7, [l^6..l^4] d_delta C5; l.1051-1063: [l^8..l^5] d_v C7,
  [l^7..l^5] K8), plus the RG second-log check below.
  * The normalized jets are rebuilt with all 439 engine coefficients (not only the 116 needed) and logs to
    l^12. Log powers above the engine's computed range at a computed eps power are exact structural zeros
    (the engine expands each Laurent series from the lowest order that can occur).
* **RG second-log check (derived here).** Write the responses as functions of the auxiliary couplings,
  F(g,h; l) = sum_k F_k(g,h) l^k (framework.md §1-2). A pure-log renormalization with an l-independent beta
  gives the Callan-Symanzik equation dF/dl = beta . grad F, hence F_{k+1} = beta . grad F_k / (k+1).
  * F_1 = beta . grad F_0 is the framework's J0 beta = R1.
  * F_2 = (1/2) beta . grad F_1 needs only first h-derivatives, i.e. d_v and E of the l^1 data and the
    normal-ordering factor N(h).
  * The framework verified l-independence at b0 = 1, delta = 0, and the check is used only there. Away from
    that point the matching eq:fugmatch adds explicit logs: the naive formula fails for d_v C3[l^2]
    (2 against -3) and d_delta C3[l^2] (0 against -2 z2).
  * At (v, delta) = (1, 0):
    C_{n,2} = 1/2 [ sum_m m b_{n-m+1} C_{m,1} + 2 sum_m h_{n-m} (-2m C_{m,0} - 2 d_v C_{m,1} + E C_{m,1}) ],
    K_{n,2} = 1/2 [ sum_m m b_{n-m+1} K_{m,1} + sum_m h_{n-m} (-2 K_{m,1} + 2(-2m K_{m,0} - 2 d_v K_{m,1} + E K_{m,1})) ].
  * It reproduces the note's C3[l^2] = 2, K4[l^2] = -6 and C5[l^2] = 95/2 - 24 z2. For C7, K6 and K8 it is a new
    prediction, compared with the engine's l^2 coefficients.
* **(iv) direct finite-cutoff comparison** (`direct_check.py`): the direct agent's GLS tool on
  * (a) the production series as delivered (only the needed coefficients: mostly INCONCLUSIVE/UNTESTABLE, as
    `direct/README.md` predicts);
  * (b) the production series completed coefficient by coefficient by poles1, kplus1 and the validation runs.
  Every flagged moment is re-tested at eps_max = 0.011, 0.016 and 0.051, and with the conv1 values.
* **Pole-check rounding floor.** Coefficients that vanish term by term carry double-double residues of ~1e-34,
  with replicate errors of ~1e-36. An absolute floor of 1e-25 is added to sigma there. "Non-trivial"
  coefficients are those whose cancelling terms have L1 size > 1e-12; they are reported separately.

## Independence

* No archive code is imported, called or copied. No archive number is used before step 3.
* The engine, framework, direct and spec components are the project's own (phase 1-2, reviewed).
* Closed forms used in step 2 are the note's proved results, taken from the archive-free
  `spec/out/validation_targets.json`.
* The claims under test enter only in step 3.
