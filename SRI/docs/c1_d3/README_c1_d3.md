# c1_d3: c_1 in d = 3 at the short-range Ising end

Near the crossover of the 3d long-range Ising model to the short-range (SR) Ising universality class,

    Delta_eps(s) = Delta_eps^SR + c_1 delta + O(delta^2),   delta = (s_* - s)/2,   s_* = 3 - 2 Delta_sigma^SR,

and c_1 = a0/b0 is a ratio of two integrated four-point functionals of the 3d Ising CFT (Behan, Rastelli, Rychkov and
Zan 2017). This package evaluates them with the retained 60-primary spectrum of Simmons-Duffin 2016 (even and odd
primaries below dimension 24) and the leading data of Chang et al. 2024, and bounds the omitted part with a crossing
linear program under stated gap assumptions. Paper: Sec. 3.2 "Near the short-range end", paragraph "Two and three dimensions", and Table tab:sr, d = 3 row.
Derivation: the SRI note, Sec. 4 and Appendix G. The package's own single-file note (`note/ising3d_C1_note.tex` and
PDF) is not shipped since 16 October 2026 (one note per direction; `public/NOT_SHIPPED.txt`); its content is in
Appendix G.
Written by OpenAI GPT-6 astra (via ChatGPT), supplied by the author (7 October 2026); reviewed by Claude Code; this is
the review-revised version of 2026-10-08 (PROVENANCE_c1_d3.md).

What the range is and is not: it assumes no unlisted Z2-even primary below dimension 8 and no unlisted
Z2-odd primary below 7 in the relevant OPEs, inputs inside twice their quoted (mostly nonrigorous) errors, the
sparse-bootstrap constraint on the epsilon-prime coupling, and sampled nonlinear and tail allowances. It is not robust
to modest changes: gaps (8, 6.5) or (7, 7), a box of three quoted errors, or a third crossing padding already reach
outside it, and the central input vector itself is infeasible in the nominal linear program (68 sigma^4 rows violated;
the inputs must move by up to 1.15 quoted errors). `make replay-full` reruns these diagnostics.

Two independent implementations agree to 10^{-12} (SRI note, Table 2; `\SRthreeConeImplDev`, which the paper does not print): `review_indep/` ships the two independent
re-implementations written for the 2026-10-07 review (one with its own Casimir-recursion blocks and quadrature, one
with blocks from the Zamolodchikov-type recursion of Kos, Poland and Simmons-Duffin and the other arrangement of the integrals of Behan et al.); neither imports the
package. The replay reruns both on the shipped inputs and requires |delta c_1| <= 1e-12 against the package and between
the two (measured on Intel and AMD: c_1 within 6e-14 and 5e-13 of the package, the two within 4e-13 of each other;
a0 and b0 within relative 2.3e-13 and 2.1e-12). The package's own two
integrated forms (`blocks.integrate`, `kernels.py`) are reported separately as a same-domain consistency check.
