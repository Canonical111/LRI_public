# Data: SRI

Everything `test` and `replay` need ships in this folder: about 220 MB unpacked, about 50 MB packed (`tar czf` of the
simulated exports). No file exceeds 50 MB; data live inside each package, there is no top-level `data/`. Per package
(details, file lists and reasons: `docs/<pkg>/DATA_<pkg>.md`):

| Package | Ships | Hosted (`make fetch-data`) | Not exported (summary) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `dual_d1` | 704 of the compendium's 8,560 files, chosen by a dependency trace of every task: 61 MB, 8.2 MB packed (largest: the C6 certificate rows of weight 5, 14 MB) | the weight-6 C7 certificate, 57,283,795 bytes, sha256 `1a310b7fe88ed5b7...` (`public/HOSTED.json`); `replay-full` only | the raw integration stages and checkpoints, raw P6 sources and period tables (about 1.9 GB), earlier packages, stage notes; all in the original archive |
| `indep_d1` | 772 files, 21 MB, 3.8 MB packed: job definitions, collected results and framework inputs of every production run, the result records, and `public/pooled_sector_record.json` (7.0 MB: the exact sufficient statistics of the raw outputs) | the raw per-sector engine outputs (293 files, 131 MB); `replay-raw` only | pilot, calibration and probe runs (about 300 MB; summarised in the reports), logs, scheduler records, the personal desktop's job scripts and ledgers |
| `integ_d1` | 733 files (724 of the project at commit 8bd5a22, 9 added), 48.6 MB, 15.9 MB packed (15.3 MiB as a git pack): the code, the frozen records with their registry and provenance sidecars, the step-0 maps, the designs and engine inputs, and the raw engine outputs every record is rebuilt from (the three gate levels of the four-bubble production are most of it) | - (nothing hosted) | the working notes (their content is the note's Sec. 2.7 and Appendix I), pilot and design runs, superseded M3 stages, gate and fix-round logs, one launch script naming the personal desktop; the review log is never shipped |
| `c2_closed_d2` | 339 files, 4.9 MB, 1.8 MB packed: the study (without its note) and the 142 Monte Carlo chunk outputs | - | the Monte Carlo binaries and job logs (the lines they printed are in `public/mc_production_logs.json`), the supplied source archive |
| `c2_cylinder_d2` | 293 files, 77 MB, 15.8 MB packed (27.6 MiB as a git pack): the 16 production and 25 extraction-check records, the auxiliary inputs, the 17 archived records, the result generation | - | 654 tracked files listed in `public/NOT_EXPORTED.txt` (earlier revisions and review evidence, the closed-form study covered by `c2_closed_d2`), code tarballs and run logs; the Cauchy grids (about 52 GB each at N = 1024; deterministic, regenerated by a production rerun, not hosted) |
| `c1_d3` | 284 files, 5.9 MB, 3.4 MB packed (with the review's two re-implementations, 21 kB) | - | 33 files of the authors' list (`public/NOT_SHIPPED.txt`; the package's note and its PDF since 2026-10-16): 25 sensitivity arrays (`replay-full` regenerates them), the review report (summarised), history |
| top level | `note/` (TeX, PDF; the only note of SRI/), `results/`, `verify/`: under 2 MB | - | the six separate notes of the packages (since 2026-10-16; their content is in the note's Appendices A to G), and, since 2026-10-17, the compendium's TeX source and the scripts that only build notes (`dual_d1`, `c2_closed_d2`, `c1_d3`; each package's `public/NOT_SHIPPED.txt`); the blocks of the compendium's TeX that the checks parse are kept in `code/dual_d1/public/compendium_extract.json` |

The two hosted files are in the project's Zenodo data record, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23466766, under flat names
(each package's `public/HOSTED.json`: name, size, sha256, URL `https://zenodo.org/records/23466766/files/<name>`): `SRI__dual_d1__C7.w6.certificate.txt`
(57,283,795 bytes, the pinned bytes of the certificate) and `SRI__indep_d1__raw_engine_outputs.tar.gz` (30,749,940 bytes,
a deterministic tar.gz of the 293 raw outputs, each checked against `RAW_SHA256SUMS.txt`). `make fetch-data` downloads,
checks and places them; `URL_BASE=<url>` points it at a mirror. Without them `replay-full` of `dual_d1` fails its
certificate row and `replay-raw` of `indep_d1` cannot run; `replay` of `indep_d1` uses its pooled record instead. The raw
outputs keep the run-folder suffix `_can` (personal desktop; author decision 2026-10-15, confirmed 2026-10-19); their
contents name no machine.

## Hash-bound and re-pinned records

Code that produced a quoted number and the records bound by checksums ship byte-identical:
- `dual_d1`: every shipped file is listed in the compendium's `reproduction/MANIFEST.json` (checked by its own
  `run_checks.py`); none needs scrubbing.
- `indep_d1`: 22 files named the personal desktop and are published scrubbed and **re-pinned** (`RE-PINNED.json`,
  `RE-PINNED.md`: original and published sha256; the machine word removed, no number changed): the C8 result records,
  their hash cascade and the documents and code that used the name. The verifiers check them against their published
  sha256. The other hash-bound records (engine deliverables, sealed comparison files, `RESULTS_independent.json`) keep
  their original bytes.
- `integ_d1`: the frozen records (registry `results/frozen_records.json`), their provenance sidecars, the engine
  outputs with their `.out.fp` fingerprints and the code files whose hashes the records carry (the gate, the step-0
  generators, the engine sources); none names a personal machine or a private path. Its six launch templates
  (`code/*.sbatch`) are not hash-bound and carry placeholders.
- `c2_closed_d2`: the study's files bound by its `MANIFEST.json`; `outputs/summary.json` is also byte-identical to the
  frozen copy in `Resummation/`.
- `c2_cylinder_d2`: the records, sidecars, task receipts, merged indexes and auxiliary inputs (sha256 checked at run
  time by the analysis); the receipts keep the cluster user names in their symmetry and SDSC Expanse paths.
- `c1_d3`: the authors' `SHA256SUMS` (checked in subset mode) and the reproduced outputs whose sha256 the release
  records carry (the records also carry the sha256 of the package's note, which is not shipped).

Files that are not hash-bound carry the repository's placeholders (`<PROJECT>`, `<SCRATCH>`, `<USER>`, `<NODE>`,
`<ACCOUNT>`, ...; `AGENTS.md`). Where a verbatim checksum list covers a scrubbed file, it is listed in the package's
`public/SCRUBBED.txt` and checked for presence only; every verifier honours `SCRUBBED.txt` and `RE-PINNED.json`.

## Names

No shipped file names a personal machine: the only machines named are the symmetry cluster and SDSC Expanse, and the
authors' own machines appear as "personal desktop". `indep_d1` keeps the directory suffix `_can` of the tier-B/C runs
made on the personal desktop. Records written in the sandbox of the other AI system name its paths (`/mnt/data/...`,
`/workspace`): history, not paths to use.

## closure_d1

The check tool and, in `prescription/`, the prescription argument's checker archive without its note (about 0.1 MB);
no numerical records, nothing hosted. The two notes are not shipped since 2026-10-16 (content in the note's Appendix D).
