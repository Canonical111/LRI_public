# Reproduce: Resummation

Environment: Python >= 3.10 with numpy, scipy, mpmath, matplotlib, sympy (`requirements.txt`). Tested with Python 3.12.9,
numpy 2.3.5, scipy 1.17.1, mpmath 1.3.0, matplotlib 3.11.2, sympy (symmetry cluster, Intel Xeon 6148 and AMD EPYC 7532),
unpacked in node-local storage. No cluster is needed: everything runs on one core.

| Step | Command (from Resummation/) | Cost | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `make test` | 20 s | snapshot sha256 lists; three baseline points of d = 1 recomputed; the 3d resummation rerun; the d = 4 benchmark; the comparison |
| 2 | `make replay` | 40 s | every \RS* and \CMP* macro and every cell of tab:num1-3 taken from this folder (86) from the shipped records (half-up rounding to the printed digits; d = 2 MC by the PDG rule); derived tables regenerated byte for byte; figure data; inputs against the sibling folders (SKIP where absent) |
| 3 | `make replay-full` | 3 min | reruns the d = 1 chain and the ten 2d/3d scripts; all outputs within 1e-12 + 1e-12 \|x\| (fits 1e-6); quoted numbers from the rerun |
| 4 | `make figures` | desktop | rebuilds the two figure PDFs with wolframscript and TeX (MaTeX); optional; not through Slurm |

Per package: `make -C code/<pkg> -f public.mk help`. Every quoted number, its record, rule and tolerance:
`results_map.md` (all packages; per package `code/<package>/public/results_map.md`) and `results/quoted_numbers.json`.

## Decisions you must not make
- The resummation prescriptions (d = 1: endpoint-v, K = 8, N = 8, b_L = 3, alpha = 0; d = 2: curvature construction,
  b_L = 3.5; d = 3: slope construction, b_L = 4), the sensitivity scans and the inputs (C8(1) final; C^SR_8; c_2; c_1 and its
  estimated range, status conditional; S, E_SR) are fixed. Do not choose a prescription by agreement with HT, NRG or MC.
- The ranges are sensitivity ranges, not confidence intervals; the method errors (HT fit half-spread, NRG linear sum,
  MC statistical error and fit-model half-range) are not standard deviations and are not combined.
- The rounding rule of the quoted numbers and the tolerances of replay-full.
