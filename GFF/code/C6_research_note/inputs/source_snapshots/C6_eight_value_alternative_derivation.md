# Optional eight-value representation of C6(1)

## What this result does and does not establish

The accepted input is the nine-value formula in `inputs/nine_value_expression.json`.
The present result is an exact expression compression. It replaces the two
multiple-polylogarithm components in that formula by one fixed classical complex
Kampé de Fériet value (followed by multiplication by an explicit complex constant
and taking a real part). Seven non-polylogarithmic values are unchanged.

It does **not** prove an eight-dimensional independent period basis. In
particular, it neither expresses D3 separately in terms of D5 and the other old
values nor proves a relation among those old nine values. The simpler-parameter
nine-value representation is retained as the default input. The alternative
introduces five roots of a specified quintic, and the known polynomial grows
from 18 to 21 monomials. Thus fewer function calls should not be confused with
an unqualified simplification.

The source identity converting the polylogarithmic block to K5 is inherited
from `C6_linear_geometric_expression.json`. The new analytic step is the finite
summation-by-parts identity below and its standard KdF coefficient identification.
All physical graph/RG identifications, all seven other special-value reductions,
and the source repair remain inherited. They are not newly verified here.

## 1. Inherited exact target and new finite value

Put
\[
 z=(1+i)/2,\quad \ell=\log2,\quad
 \kappa=3\pi^2/64+\pi\ell/8-G/2,\quad
 c=3\ell/2+3i\pi/4,\quad G=\operatorname{Catalan}.
\]
The inherited geometric target is
\[
 K_5=\Re L,\qquad
 L=\sum_{n\ge1}z^n\left[
 \frac{H_{n-1}^{(2)}+i\kappa}{n^3}+\frac c{n^4}+\frac3{n^5}
 \right].
\]
The sum is a proof/intermediate expression, not a residual in the new formula.

Define the degree-five polynomial
\[
 P(x)=[i\kappa(x+1)^2+c(x+1)+3](x+2)^3
 -z[i\kappa(x+2)^2+c(x+2)+2](x+1)^3.
\]
Its leading coefficient is \(i\kappa(1-z)=\kappa z\ne0\). Define the unordered
five-member multiset by
\[
 P(x)=\kappa z\prod_{j=1}^5(x+\beta_j).
\]
Every root is included with multiplicity. The parameters are algebraic over the
field generated by the displayed known constants, not asserted algebraic over
\(\mathbb Q\). No unknown C6 period occurs in their specification.

Set
\[
 \Omega=\frac{zP(0)}8,
\]
\[
 \mathcal F=F_{8:0;2}^{8:1;3}\!\left[
 \begin{matrix}1^{[3]},1+\boldsymbol\beta:1;1^{[3]}\\
 3^{[3]},\boldsymbol\beta:-;2^{[2]}\end{matrix};z,z\right].
\]
Here repeated parameters and vector addition have their usual literal meanings.
The notation is the classical complex-variable Kampé de Fériet function. Its
coefficient of \(x^m y^n\), for shared upper/lower lists A/D and local lists B/E,
C/F, is
\[
 \frac{(A)_{m+n}(B)_m(C)_n}{(D)_{m+n}(E)_m(F)_n m!n!}.
\]
This is the convention in the introduction of Ito–Kumabe–Nakagawa–Nemoto,
*Kampé de Fériet hypergeometric functions over finite fields*, arXiv:2212.14321v2.
Only their classical complex-variable definition is used, not a finite-field
formula or a claim that their paper evaluates C6.

The new identity is
\[
 \boxed{L=\Omega\mathcal F,\qquad K_5=\Re(\Omega\mathcal F).}
\]

## 2. Exact finite Abel identity

For \(k\ge0\), put
\[
 u_k=\frac{z^{k+1}}{(k+1)^3},\quad J_k=H_k^{(2)},\quad
 h_k=\frac1{(k+1)^2},\quad
 \xi_k=i\kappa+\frac c{k+1}+\frac3{(k+1)^2}.
\]
Define
\[
 V_k=u_k(\xi_k/h_k-1),\qquad w_k=u_k+V_k-V_{k+1}.
\]
Since \(J_{k+1}-J_k=h_k\) and \(J_0=0\), finite summation by parts gives, for
**every** positive integer N,
\[
 \boxed{\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}u_k(J_k+\xi_k)
 -\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}w_kJ_{k+1}=V_NJ_N.}
\]
The identity follows by substituting the definition of w and collecting adjacent
V terms. It has no missing lower-boundary contribution. In particular the
original n=1 term has not been discarded.

Because \(|z|=1/\sqrt2<1\), \(V_N=O(|z|^N/N)\) and \(J_N<\zeta(2)\). The boundary
therefore vanishes. Direct rational collection gives
\[
 w_k=\frac{z^{k+1}P(k)}{(k+1)^3(k+2)^3},\qquad w_0=\Omega.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \frac{w_k}{\Omega}=z^k\frac{(1)_k^3}{(3)_k^3}
 \prod_{j=1}^5\frac{(1+\beta_j)_k}{(\beta_j)_k}.
\]
The local KdF coefficient is exactly
\[
 \frac{(1)_j^3}{(2)_j^2j!}=\frac1{(j+1)^2}.
\]
Grouping the standard defining coefficients by k=m+j therefore supplies
\(J_{k+1}\), and establishes \(L=\Omega\mathcal F\).

This identifies a member of an established function family independently of the
value L. It is still representation compression, not a proof that the separate
original constants are algebraically dependent.

## 3. Admissibility and convergence

The elementary bounds \(\pi>3\), \(\ell>2/3\), \(0<G<1\) imply
\(\kappa>11/64>0\). Also \(\kappa<1\) and \(|c|<4\).
The coefficients of \(\Re P(x)\), in descending powers from x^5 to x^0, are
\[
\begin{split}
&\kappa/2,\quad 3\ell/4+7\kappa/2+3\pi/8,\\
&27\ell/4+19\kappa/2+15\pi/8+2,\\
&81\ell/4+25\kappa/2+27\pi/8+15,\\
&99\ell/4+8\kappa+21\pi/8+33,\\
&21\ell/2+2\kappa+3\pi/4+23.
\end{split}
\]
All are positive. Thus P(x) cannot vanish for real x>=0. No beta_j is a
nonpositive integer; P(0) and Omega are nonzero. This is an exact argument,
not a conclusion from numerical root samples.

The shared normalized coefficient is O(1/k), and the absolute sum of the local
coefficients over any diagonal is less than zeta(2). The double series at (z,z)
therefore converges absolutely. For the exact N-term diagonal sum of
\(\Omega\mathcal F\), a conservative elementary tail bound is
\[
 |\Omega\mathcal F-\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}w_kJ_{k+1}|
 \le \frac{30 |z|^{N+1}}{(N+1)(1-|z|)}.
\]
This bounds truncation in exact arithmetic only, not rounding in the numerical
script. It follows from \(|w_k|\le15|z|^{k+1}/(k+1)\) and \(J_{k+1}<2\).

## 4. Complete coefficient substitution

Let E be the current accepted known polynomial, and
\[
 B_{\rm pol}=4352[(32G+9\pi^2)D_3-64D_5].
\]
The inherited linear-geometric reduction gives
\[
 E+B_{\rm pol}=E_8+835584K_5.
\]
Therefore the optional new formula replaces exactly those two blocks by
\[
 \boxed{E_8+835584\Re(\Omega\mathcal F).}
\]
The entire new polynomial E8 is printed in FORMULA.md and in the output JSON.
Its shift from the current E (not from an older intermediate) is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 E_8-E={}&\frac{221}{80}A^5-\frac{221}{16}A^4\pi
 -\frac{1717}{24}A^3\pi^2-816A^2G\pi
 +\frac{2771}{8}A^2\pi^3+16626A^2\zeta(3)\\
 &-3264AG\pi^2-\frac{377077}{240}A\pi^4
 -33252A\pi\zeta(3)-\frac{21488}{3}G\pi^3
 +\frac{534089}{240}\pi^5\\
 &-\frac{326978}{3}\pi^2\zeta(3)+1228896\zeta(5).
\end{aligned}
\]
The Q-dependent block is unchanged. The source repair remains included once,
and neither wheel nor primitive zero modes are separated.

The symbolic verification also reconstructs this inherited weighted identity
from the retained D3/S5/T3 transformations, rather than comparing only selected
monomials. It does not newly prove their upstream HPL functional identities.

## 5. Executed checks and interpretation

`verify.py` performs 40 finite exact checks. These include the finite Abel
boundary identity for N=1,...,12, the general shared coefficient ratio, the local
KdF coefficients, parameter admissibility, and the complete polynomial change.

`numerical.py` independently evaluates:
1. the original geometric harmonic coefficients;
2. the new rational Abel coefficients;
3. the root-specified KdF coefficients;
4. D3 and D5 through their own HPL series at i/2 and Taylor continuation to i.

Two runs use 60 and 110 working decimal digits, respectively 700 and 1200 terms
per route. At 110 digits the old/new complete weighted substitution differs by
4.470e-105. The root-based and original geometric values differ by 6.858e-111.
These are floating-point checks, **not** new rigorous special-value interval
certificates. No all-sector C6 evaluation or Wolfram kernel was run.

The result removes one written call, not one proved independent generator.
The field of coefficients includes the known pi, log(2), Catalan, gamma and
zeta constants. An eight-call expression for one particular combination does
not show that all of the old nine values lie in an eight-dimensional span over
that field. The original nine-call formula is retained for simpler parameters.
