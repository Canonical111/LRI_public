Sixth-order long-range Ising predictions vs. bootstrap

Behan, Lauria, Nocchi and van Vliet, arXiv:2311.02742v6. Interactive overlays on the original figure images when online; explicitly labelled approximate tracings when offline.

No bootstrap or short-range endpoint data were fitted. The ordinary Borel [2/2] approximation of a/ε² has a positive-real-axis pole in both 2D and 3D and was rejected. The two plotted pole-free alternatives are illustrative, not controlled confidence bounds. All loop coefficients are inherited from the preceding calculation, not independently rederived for this plot. Comparing a resummation to a plotted bound does not by itself rule out the perturbative coefficients.
