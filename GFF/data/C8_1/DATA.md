# GFF/data/C8_1: records of the C8(1) production (one dimension, full eighth order)

Everything here is in git (about 155 MB, 1,675 files, no file above 1 MB except `inputs8/L8_coord.txt.xz`,
10.6 MB, and the four pilot checkpoint files of 4.8 MB each). Nothing is hosted elsewhere. `MANIFEST.sha256` lists every file; check with
`cd GFF/data/C8_1 && shasum -a 256 -c MANIFEST.sha256`.

| Path | What | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `c8_hpc/runs/prod4/b1..b4/` | batches 1-4 (SDSC Expanse, AMD EPYC 7742), per campaign `manifest.json` and `merged/` | 27 MB |
| `c8_hpc/runs/ext1/b5..b14/` | batches 5-14 (b5-b11 symmetry Intel Xeon Gold 6148, b12-b14 Expanse); `extension.json` (amendment A1 record), `extension_A1close.json` (A1-close inclusion set) | 68 MB |
| `c8_hpc/runs/exact8_L5/` | the deterministic loop-five campaign (degrees 6-14) with its inputs | 2 MB |
| `c8_hpc/runs/tailtest_L7/` | the complete 4096-points-per-graph seven-loop test of the pilot (cited in note Section 30.3), checkpoints included | 21 MB |
| `c8_hpc/runs/pilot8/` | the two seven-loop power-free campaigns of the pilot, `tprodA_L7` and `tprodB_L7` (64 points per graph, 16 replicates; cited in note Section 30.3), complete with checkpoints, task plans and Sobol nets, and the pilot's batch JSON; the other 28 pilot campaigns are not included | 21 MB |
| `c8_hpc/analysis/v1/` | `affine8.json`, `full_affine8.json` (the frozen affine map with the zero-pole controls) | 64 KB |
| `c8_hpc/designs_frozen/prod4_34000/` | the frozen production design (design, unit, per-graph counts, design_meta) | 1.9 MB |
| `inputs8/L8_coord.txt.xz` | nine-vertex coordinate inventory (234 MB decompressed, sha256 2288143f...) | 10.6 MB |

The layout under `c8_hpc/` is the production tree on the symmetry cluster (`<SCRATCH>/c8_hpc/`), so the
relative paths recorded in the published results (`../../runs/prod4/b1/...`) resolve. The records are byte-identical
copies; each merged stream is bound by sha256 to its campaign audit and to the published `C8_final.json`.

Not included (not needed to reproduce a quoted number):
- the 83,380 checkpoint files of the 14 batches (about 34 GB). Each `merged/audit.json` lists their sha256 values.
  `make replay` checks every merged output against its audit but does not re-validate the checkpoints. Full
  re-validation needs the checkpoints, which remain on the symmetry cluster;
- the task plans and Sobol nets (regenerated from the frozen design and the seeds), the per-batch copies of the inputs
  and the generated quadrature weights (regenerated by `cluster/weights8.slurm`; their sha256 values are in every
  manifest), the other 28 pilot campaigns and the Slurm logs.
